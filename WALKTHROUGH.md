## 1. GETTING STARTED

Pick Fighter. Pick Lethis for your god. Pick traits that add STR
or CON. Start the game.

Fighter has 35 HP, parries 25% of incoming hits (counter-attacks
for double), and is hard to screw up. Lethis lets you survive one
lethal hit per floor and makes rest heal 100% instead of the
usual depth-scaled amount. You will not find a safer first run.


## 2. THORNWALL AND THE BARROW

You spawn near Thornwall. Walk into town. Bump into the elder to
get your first quest: kill the Barrow Wight in The Barrow, east
of town.

Buy a weapon from the shopkeeper if you can. Equip it (I key,
select, E to equip). Head east until you find stairs down.

```
THE BARROW (warrens, 3 floors)
  Floor 1: rats, bats, kobolds. You one-shot all of them.
  Floor 2: same but tougher. Spiders show up. They poison.
           Poison ticks for small damage. Not dangerous yet.
  Floor 3: BARROW WIGHT
           45 HP, 8 damage, 3 armor.
           Hits hard. Rest before fighting if you're hurt.
           Lethis will save you from one killing blow.
           If it triggers, rest and re-engage.
```

Kill the Wight. Go back to Thornwall. Talk to Scholar Aldric.
He tells you about three fragments.


## 3. THE THREE FRAGMENTS

You need all three before you can enter the Sepulchre. The quest
chain is linear. You do them in this order:

```
STONEKEEP (stonekeep zone, 4 floors)
  Northeast of Greywatch.
  Enemies: orcs, goblins, goblin archers, bandits.
  Archers shoot at range. Close distance.
  BOSS: THE WARDEN (floor 4)
        65 HP, 9 damage, 4 armor. Basic melee fighter.
        Nothing special. Hit him until he falls.
  Pick up the fragment in the same room.
```

Talk to Sage Yeva in Frostmere (north). She gives you the next
two locations.

```
THE CATACOMBS (catacombs zone, 4 floors)
  Near Millhaven, south of Thornwall.
  Enemies: skeletons (2 armor), zombies, ghouls, wraiths.
  Wraiths phase through walls. Normal weapons pass through
  them. You need silver, mithril, or adamantine to hit them.
  If you don't have any, run past.
  BOSS: THE REVENANT KING (floor 4)
        80 HP, 11 damage, 3 armor. Necromancer AI.
        Raises nearby corpses as allies. Kill his summons
        or they pile up. Focus him down fast.

THE MOLTEN DEPTHS (molten zone, 4 floors)
  Beneath Ironhearth, east side of the map.
  Enemies: manticores, basilisks, gargoyles, imps.
  Basilisks can blind you for 5 turns on a gaze attack.
  BOSS: THE SLAG MOTHER (floor 4)
        100 HP, 14 damage, 5 armor. Dragon AI.
        Breathes fire at range 2-4. Either get adjacent
        (range 1) or stay beyond range 4.
        Hardest of the three guardians.
```


## 4. HOLLOWGATE

```
THE HOLLOWGATE (deep halls zone, 4 floors)
  West side of the map, in the Greenwood.
  Enemies: trolls, ogres, golems, yetis.
  Trolls regen 2 HP/turn. Setting them on fire (burn
  status) stops the regen.
  Break the seal on floor 4 with your three fragments.
  The Sepulchre opens.
```


## 5. THE SEPULCHRE

Nine floors. Bring potions. Bring your best gear.

```
FLOORS 1-2: Undead. Skeletons, zombies, wraiths, ghouls.
            Same rules as Catacombs. Silver for wraiths.

FLOOR 3:    THE BONE COLOSSUS
            120 HP, 14 damage, 6 armor. Charger AI.
            Rushes you from 2-4 tiles away. Stuns on
            impact and deals bonus damage. Don't give
            it a running start. Stay adjacent or stay
            far away.

FLOORS 4-6: Fire and eldritch. Manticores, basilisks,
            gargoyles, golems.

FLOOR 6:    THE EMBER WYRM
            160 HP, 20 damage, 5 armor. Dragon AI.
            Fire breath. Same as Slag Mother but meaner.
            Use your potions.

FLOORS 7-8: Eldritch. Wraiths, liches, death knights.
            Liches teleport when hurt and drain your HP
            at range. Death knights have a fear aura.
            These are the hardest regular floors.

FLOOR 9:    THE KEEPER (final boss)
            250 HP, 25 damage, 8 armor.
            Three phases:

            Phase 1 (100-50% HP):
              Ranged attack at range 5, 8 damage.
              Close to melee range to avoid it.

            Phase 2 (50-25% HP):
              "THE KEEPER SHEDS ITS ARMOR."
              Faster. Lower armor. Harder hits.

            Phase 3 (below 25%):
              "THE RELIQUARY SPEAKS THROUGH IT."
              Deals 2 damage/turn if you're adjacent.
              Speed 100. Very fast.
              Burn him down. Use capstone abilities,
              potions, whatever you have left.

            Kill it. Pick up the Reliquary. You win.
```


## 6. CLASS PICKS

Four base classes. Twenty more unlock through play.

```
FIGHTER  35 HP, 4 dmg. Parries 25% of hits for 2x counter.
         Swords boost parry to 40%. Lv5: 3 guaranteed parries.
         Best for: learning the game.

ROGUE    25 HP, 3 dmg. First hit on uninjured target teleports
         behind them, deals 2x. Daggers remove the "uninjured"
         restriction. Lv5: re-stealth after kills (3-turn CD).
         Best for: burst damage, experienced players.

WIZARD   20 HP, 2 dmg. Spells scale with INT. Staves give
         bonus casts. Lv5: spend 2x MP for 3x spell damage.
         Best for: AoE damage, fragile.

RANGER   28 HP, 3 dmg. First target hit is marked, all hits
         on marked target +50% (75% with bow). Lv5: marked
         targets can't regen.
         Best for: consistent single-target damage.
```


## 7. GOD PICKS

```
LETHIS    Easiest. Survive one lethal blow per floor.
          Rest always heals 100%. PER halved (traps harder
          to spot).

SOLETH    Straightforward. +3 fire on all attacks.
          You take 2x fire damage yourself.

MORRETH   First hit per fight does 2x. +3 armor.
          Cannot use ranged weapons at all.

GATHRUUN  +5 armor, +4 damage underground. Slower on
          the surface. Best pure dungeon god.

YASHKHET  Hard mode. 15% lifesteal but you CANNOT heal
          from rest, potions, or spells. Damage is your
          only healing.

IXUUL     Chaos. Immune to all status effects. Stats
          randomly shift over time. Shops cost 2x.
          No shrines work for you.
```

Pick a god whose tenets match how you play. Tenets are
rules (don't kill animals, don't use ranged, etc). Break
them and you lose favor. Follow them and favor builds.
75 favor unlocks a mastery prayer.


## 8. SPELLS WORTH LEARNING

Spell tomes drop in dungeons. Even melee classes should grab:

```
Minor Heal     4 MP    Emergency heal.
Phase         10 MP    Teleport anywhere visible. Escape button.
Reveal Map     8 MP    Shows the whole floor. Finds traps.
```

For casters:

```
Fireball      10 MP    20 + INT/3 damage. Burns. Your bread
                       and butter.
Lightning     12 MP    25 + INT/3 damage. Range 8. Best reach.
Meteor        20 MP    35 + INT/3 damage. Drops in molten zones.
Earthquake    15 MP    AoE. Drops in deep halls.
```


## 9. PASSIVE TREE PRIORITIES

You get one point per level. 260+ nodes in 8 sectors. Don't
scatter points. Pick a direction.

```
FIGHTER: Might -> Fortitude.
  Target: Unbreakable capstone (halve all damage, 8 turns).
  Pick up HP and armor nodes on the way.

ROGUE: Finesse -> Shadow.
  Target: Death Mark capstone (guaranteed 3x crit).
  Pick up crit and stealth nodes.

WIZARD: Arcane -> Faith.
  Target: Arcane Overload capstone (0 MP + 2x power).
  Pick up spell damage and MP nodes.

RANGER: Finesse -> Nature.
  Target: Aspect of Beast capstone (transform, +5 all stats).
  Pick up ranged damage and PER nodes.
```

Keystones are trade-offs at sector boundaries. Read them
before allocating. Blood Magic (spells cost HP, +30% power)
and Ghost Blade (melee scales INT) are build-defining.


## 10. HEALING AND REST

```
REST (dungeons):
  2 per floor. 3 with Lethis or Farmer background.
  Can't rest with enemies visible.
  Heals a percentage of max HP:
    Depth 1: 80%
    Depth 2: 70%
    Depth 3: 60%
    Depth 4+: 50%
  Lethis always heals 100%.
  MP always fully restores.

POTIONS:
  Use from inventory. Instant heal.
  Names are random each run. Drink to identify,
  or use the Identify spell.

SHRINES:
  God shrines in dungeons. If it matches your god:
  +5 favor, small heal, identify equipped items.
```

## 12. COMMON DEATHS AND HOW TO AVOID THEM

```
"Killed by wraith"
  Your weapon passes through them. Get silver or
  mithril before entering catacombs or sepulchre.

"Killed by lich"
  They drain HP at range and teleport when hurt.
  Corner them or nuke with a big spell. Don't chase
  them around the room trading drain hits.

"Killed by The Slag Mother"
  You stood at range 2-4 (breath range). Get to
  range 1 or stay at range 5+.

"Killed by The Keeper (Phase 3)"
  Phase 3 is a DPS check. If you don't have potions
  or a capstone ability ready, you're probably dead.
  Save Unbreakable/Overload/Death Mark for this.

"Killed by poison"
  Stack poison from spiders or naga can add up.
  Antidote potions or Cure Poison spell. Don't
  ignore it at low HP.

"Ran out of rests on floor 4"
  You only get 2 per floor. Don't burn them early.
  Rest when below 40% HP, not before.
```
